KeepSaying is a simple speaking practice tool for struggling beginners —
people who need basic words and simple sentences to actually stay in their
head, not another flashcard app.

You keep saying the phrases that matter to you until they stick. That's the
whole idea, and that's the point: practice that survives contact with a busy
day.

## Where it stands

Live at [keepsaying.com](https://keepsaying.com) and actively developed. If
you're learning a language and the basics keep slipping away, try it — and
tell me what's missing.
