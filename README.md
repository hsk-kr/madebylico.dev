# madebylico.com

The place where I, **Lico**, show my ideas to the world — as working demos,
not pitch decks.

## Why this exists

I have far more ideas than I could ever launch as real services. Turning
every one of them into a full product — with billing, support, marketing,
maintenance — would mean most of them never leave my head.

So I do the next best thing: build the demo, put it in front of people, and
see who reacts. Every project on [madebylico.com](https://madebylico.com) is
an idea made just real enough to try. Some will grow into something bigger.
Some won't. But all of them are out in the open, where something can
actually happen to them.

And if one of them leads somewhere — a user, a collaboration, an
opportunity — that's exactly what this site is for.

## Take an idea

Everything on the site is public, finished or not. If an idea clicks with
you:

- **Use it** — try the demo, break it, tell me what's missing.
- **Build it** — you don't need my permission. An idea in a notebook helps
  no one; an idea in the open at least has a chance.
- **Tell me it deserves to grow** — the projects people actually respond to
  are the ones that get built further.
- **Work on it with me** — if you'd rather build it together, even better.
