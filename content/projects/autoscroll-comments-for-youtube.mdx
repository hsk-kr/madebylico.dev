Watch the video. Read the comments. At the same time.

AutoScroll Comments is a Chrome extension that shows YouTube's comment
section in a side panel next to the player, drifting through the comments at
a calm, readable pace — no more scrolling down, losing the video, and
scrolling back up.

When a comment catches your eye, hover over the panel and the scrolling
pauses instantly; like, reply, or expand threads as usual, and it resumes
when you move away. Five adjustable speeds, auto-pause while the video is
paused, and it gets out of the way entirely in fullscreen. These are
YouTube's real comments, not a copy — replies, likes, and sorting all work
exactly like the normal page.

No data collection, no tracking, no network requests. The only permission is
`storage`, used to remember your scroll speed.

## Where it stands

Packaged at v0.1.0 and headed for the Chrome Web Store. Until it lands
there, grab it from the repo, run `npm run build`, and load the `dist/`
folder as an unpacked extension.
